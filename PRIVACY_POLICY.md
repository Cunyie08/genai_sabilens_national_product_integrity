## 1. Introduction

SabiLens ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all users of our product integrity verification platform. ## 4. Personal Data We Collect

### 4.1 Data You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored in hashed form) when you register as a consumer or business user.
- **Product Scan Submissions:** Product images uploaded for authenticity verification.
- **Product Reports:** Descriptions, category selections, and any additional details you submit when reporting a suspected counterfeit product.

### 4.2 Data Collected Automatically

- **Location Data:** Geolocation (latitude and longitude) collected at the time of a product scan to determine where products are purchased and to generate geographic counterfeit-risk data. Location data is requested with your consent and can be declined.
- **Purchase Channel:** The retail channel (e.g., open market, supermarket, online) you indicate at the point of scan.
- **Device and Usage Data:** IP address, device type, browser type, operating system, and interaction logs collected for security monitoring and service improvement.

### 4.3 Data Generated by Our Platform

- **Scan Records:** AI-generated verdicts (Authentic, Suspicious, Fake), confidence scores, and the evidence package (OCR text, visual analysis signals, regulatory database match results) associated with each product scan.
- **Aggregated Analytics:** Non-identifiable statistical data on counterfeit patterns, product categories, and geographic distribution of fake goods.

---

## 5. Special Categories of Personal Data

We do not intentionally collect **sensitive personal data** as defined in **Section 30 of the NDPA 2023** (including health data, biometric data, religious beliefs, or political opinions). If any such data is inadvertently submitted within a product image, it will not be processed or stored for any purpose beyond the immediate scan transaction.

---

## 6. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

1. **Product Verification Service:** To process product images through our AI pipeline (OCR, visual analysis, and NAFDAC regulatory database matching) and return an authenticity verdict.
2. **Account Management:** To create and manage your user account and authenticate your sessions.
3. **Consumer Protection Reporting:** To compile anonymised counterfeit product reports shared with relevant Nigerian regulatory agencies (NAFDAC, FCCPC, SON) in the public interest.
4. **Platform Improvement:** To improve the accuracy of our AI models using aggregated, anonymised scan data.
5. **Safety and Fraud Prevention:** To detect and prevent abuse, fraudulent scan submissions, and misuse of our platform.
6. **Legal Compliance:** To respond to lawful requests from Nigerian government agencies, courts, and regulators.
7. **Communications:** To send you service-related notifications and, with your consent, product safety alerts relevant to your region.

---

## 7. Image Data and AI Processing

When you submit a product image for scanning:

- The image is processed in real time by our AI pipeline (OCR, object detection, and visual verification models).
- A copy of the image is uploaded to our cloud image storage (Cloudinary) and associated with your scan record for audit, dispute resolution, and regulatory reporting purposes.
- Images are **not used to identify you personally**. They are linked to scan records by a scan ID, not directly to your identity in public-facing reports.
- You may request deletion of your scan images under your rights described in Section 11.

---

## 8. Location Data

Location data (latitude and longitude) is collected only at the point of a product scan with your informed consent. We use this data to:

- Identify geographic hotspots for counterfeit products.
- Generate risk maps shared with Nigerian consumer protection agencies.
- Improve the relevance of scan results.

You may decline location access in your device settings. Declining will not prevent you from using the core scan service.

## 9. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

### 9.1 Service Providers (Data Processors)

We engage third-party service providers who process data on our behalf under binding data processing agreements consistent with **Article 4.1(8) of the NDPR 2019** and **Section 29 of the NDPA 2023**:

- **Cloud Infrastructure:** Supabase (database hosting)
- **Image Storage:** Cloudinary (product image storage)
- **AI Services:** On-premise/self-hosted AI models (no third-party AI API is used for image content)

All processors are contractually bound to process data only on our documented instructions and to implement appropriate security measures.

### 9.2 Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose **anonymised or aggregated** counterfeit product data to:

- National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC)
- Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC)
- Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)

We will only disclose **personally identifiable** information to these agencies in response to a **valid legal request, court order, or statutory obligation** under Nigerian law.

### 9.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your data where required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of SabiLens, our users, or the public.

### 9.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal data may be transferred. We will provide notice before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers — Supabase (database hosting) and Cloudinary (image storage) — are domiciled and operate infrastructure outside Nigeria. As a result, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions outside Nigeria.

In accordance with **Section 43 of the NDPA 2023**, we rely on the adequacy of the data protection legal frameworks in the countries where these providers are domiciled as the transfer mechanism. Both Supabase and Cloudinary operate under robust regulatory regimes (including GDPR where applicable) that provide a level of protection substantially equivalent to the protections afforded under Nigerian law.

We have assessed these frameworks and are satisfied that your personal data receives adequate protection in those jurisdictions. We will continue to monitor developments in the legal frameworks of our providers' countries of data residence and take remedial action if adequacy can no longer be relied upon.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, in accordance with **Section 38 of the NDPA 2023**:

|---|---|

## 13. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures consistent with **Section 39 of the NDPA 2023** and **Article 4.1(4) of the NDPR 2019**, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
- Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms.
- Role-based access controls limiting internal access to personal data.
- Regular security assessments of our platform and AI pipeline.
- Secure API authentication for all backend services.

In the event of a **data breach** that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** within **72 hours** and affected individuals without undue delay, as required by **Section 40 of the NDPA 2023**.

## 16. AI-Generated Decisions and Human Oversight

Our platform uses AI to generate product authenticity verdicts. These AI outputs are:

- **Advisory in nature** — they are provided to assist consumer decision-making, not to serve as definitive legal determinations.
- Subject to **human review** upon request. If you believe a scan verdict is incorrect, you may submit a dispute to **support@sabilens.com** for manual review.

We are committed to the principles of **fairness, explainability, and non-discrimination** in our AI systems and continuously audit our models for accuracy across product categories and regions.
